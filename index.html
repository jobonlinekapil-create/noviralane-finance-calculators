Make your money assumptions visible.

Two small calculators for freelance pricing and cash runway. Use one currency throughout. Your numbers stay in this browser.

Freelance hourly floor

Add your annual income target, business costs and reserve, then divide by realistic billable hours.

Enter your figures and calculate.

Cash runway

Estimate how many months available cash covers your monthly net outflow at a steady pace.

Enter your figures and calculate.

How the estimates work: hourly floor = (annual income target + annual costs + annual reserve) ÷ annual billable hours. Runway = available cash ÷ (monthly outflow − monthly inflow), when outflow exceeds inflow.

General education only. Local tax and legal rules are not calculated. Review your assumptions and consult a qualified professional when needed.
